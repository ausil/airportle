About Airportle

Airportle is a free daily word game where you guess a real IATA airport code — the three-letter code used on airline tickets and baggage tags, like LAX for Los Angeles or LHR for London Heathrow — in six tries. A new airport code is revealed every day, and everyone around the world gets the same puzzle.

How to play

Type a valid three-letter airport code and press Enter. The tiles change color to show how close you got:

  • Green — correct letter in the correct position.
  • Yellow — correct letter in the wrong position.
  • Gray — the letter is not in the code.

For example, if the answer is LAX and you guess LAG, the L turns green, the A turns yellow, and the G turns gray.

Two difficulty modes

Filtered draws answers from about 3,000 airports with scheduled commercial passenger service — the best place to start. Original draws from all 9,000+ IATA codes, including small, remote, and obscure airfields. Both modes reset daily and keep separate statistics.

Frequently asked questions

Is Airportle free?
Yes. Airportle is free to play in any browser, with no account required. Your streak and statistics are saved on your device.
When does the next puzzle arrive?
A new airport code puzzle unlocks every day at midnight in your local time zone. The countdown timer in the statistics panel shows when the next one is coming.
What counts as a valid guess?
Any currently assigned three-letter IATA airport code — over 9,000 of them — is accepted as a guess, in both modes.
Can I play both modes on the same day?
Yes. Filtered and Original have separate daily puzzles, streaks, and guess distributions, so you can play two puzzles a day.
